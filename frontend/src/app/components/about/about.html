What is EkubCircle?

EkubCircle is a digital ledger for the traditional Ethiopian rotating savings circle — Ekub. We bring this centuries-old community savings practice into a modern, transparent, and easy-to-use mobile platform.

How it works

A group of people agree on a fixed contribution amount. Every round, all members pay into the pot and one member takes the full pot. The circle continues until every member has received once.

EkubCircle records every payment and payout — enforcing the rules automatically so the organizer can focus on the community, not the bookkeeping.

Our Mission

To digitize Ethiopia's most trusted community savings system — making it transparent, accessible, and fair for everyone.
